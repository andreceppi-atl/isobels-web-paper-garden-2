## Snowfield

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: seamless 2D side-scroller environment plate for the Snowfield region of a web game

Primary request: Turn the supplied map blueprint into the finished snowy mountain pixel-art environment while preserving its playable composition.

Input image: strict structural map reference. Preserve the broad horizontal platform rhythm, open swing spaces, lower ground band, mountain ascent, and blank billboard locations. Do not copy its labels.

Scene: a wide snowy mountain pass with distant mountain ranges, sparse black pine trees, snowy ledges, a frozen radio trail, gentle snow particles, and open sky for web swinging.

Style: authentic low-resolution vintage pixel art, early monochrome handheld/PictoChat spirit, crisp 1-pixel and 2-pixel black linework, off-white paper background, restrained pale ice-blue fills only on tangible snowy surfaces.

Constraints: strict 16:9 side view; readable silhouettes; blank billboards; no UI, readable text, logos, watermark, or player character; no painterly rendering, smooth vector curves, realistic 3D, isometric perspective, soft gradients, or dense scenery.
