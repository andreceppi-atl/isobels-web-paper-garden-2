# Isobel's Overworld / level concepts

The production map is one continuous **76,800 × 7,680** side-scrolling world. It is intentionally larger than the old 24,000 × 2,400 Long Garden in both dimensions, while preserving its physics as a movement test bed.

## The continuous route

1. **Snowfield** — distant mountain silhouettes, pine terraces, falling snow, spider footprints, and a high white pass.
2. **Blossom Crown** — a gradual snow-to-petal transition and one enormous central cherry tree. Its crown and vertical trunk are a traversable landmark; Isobel lives at the base.
3. **Live Web** — blue links, bulletin-board windows, audio-bar shapes, and paper page fragments become physical architecture. The route descends through the **Microblog Ravine** before climbing to the old paper edge.

Seven blank, in-world billboard frames are included as future placements without turning the current build into an ad-filled world.
